About 00212.com

“00 212” is how you dial Morocco from China and most of the world. We turned that number into a gateway.

Our mission

Make it easier, safer and cheaper for people and companies to connect China and Morocco — whether they are investing in a factory, importing a container, planning a honeymoon, looking for a job, or wondering who just called from +212.

What we do

  • Publish practical, fact-checked guides in plain language.
  • Build free tools: number checker, clocks, converters and calculators.
  • Introduce requesters to vetted specialists (free for requesters).
  • Run creator contests and a talent network.

How we make money

Advertising (including Google AdSense), sponsorships, paid directory listings, job posts, referral fees from specialists, and reader donations. Sponsored content is always labelled. Commercial relationships never change our safety advice.

Editorial standards

We cite primary or reputable sources, date our updates, correct errors quickly and separate news from sponsored content. Found a mistake? Use the contact form with “Correction” in the message.

Independence

00212.com is an independent publication. We are not a government body, embassy, telecom operator, port authority or listed company, and we are not affiliated with any of them.
