## Part 1 — Research: what "00212" means in Chinese culture, the economy and people's lives

### 1.1 Cultural meaning (the part people get wrong)
- **00212 has no traditional meaning in Chinese numerology.** Codes like 8 (发, prosperity), 6 (顺, smoothness), 520 (我爱你, "I love you") and 1314 (一生一世, "forever") do. 00212 does not.
- How it reads: 零零二幺二 (*líng líng èr yāo èr*). In phone numbers, 1 is read *yāo*.
- The individual digits have mildly positive associations. 2 (二) suggests pairs and harmony (好事成双, "good things come in pairs"), and 0 (零) suggests wholeness and a new start. Online, 二 can also mean "silly". Neither reading gives you a brand hook.
- **Verdict: selling "lucky number" symbolism would be a weak, easily disproved angle. Don't build on it.**

### 1.2 What 00212 actually is: a phone route with a lot of traffic
- **00 is China's international access prefix, and 212 is Morocco's ITU country code.** Dialled from mainland China, and from Europe, India and most of the world, **00212 = a call to Morocco**. This is the domain's real value: it is a literal, globally recognised sequence.
- **Search demand:** "+212 / 00212 which country", "+212 missed call" and "+212 scam" draw large, evergreen worldwide search volume. That is because +212 numbers are widely used in wangiri (one-ring) and WhatsApp scams, and consumer-safety sites cover the code heavily.
- **A trademark trap to avoid:** HKEX stock code **00212 is Nanyang Holdings Ltd** (Hong Kong). The site must not use that company's name, look or financial context. The disclosure on the site says so.

### Positioning
**00212.com — "Dial into Morocco." The independent China ⇄ Morocco gateway.**
- **Traffic engine (mass, global):** a free +212 number checker, dialling guide, China–Morocco clocks, a CNY⇄MAD converter and scam alerts. These capture evergreen "+212 / 00212" search demand, and every visitor is shown the gateway.
- **Money engine (niche, high-ticket):** investment and company setup, trade and sourcing (RFQ), freight and customs, visa-free travel, jobs and talent. Each path ends in the **Get Matched** lead desk.

### Revenue model (in priority order)
1. **Lead brokerage.** Company-setup, customs and sourcing leads sell for roughly $50–$500+ each in B2B referral markets; retainer and referral deals with firms are also possible.
2. **Directory tiers.** Free / Featured $49 a month / Verified $149 a month.
3. **Sponsorships.** Section sponsor from $900 a month, title partner custom, contest prizes, newsletter slots.
4. **Google AdSense.** Finance, B2B and travel pages carry higher RPM than tool pages. House ads run until approval.
5. **YouTube.** A facade-embedded video hub on the site that feeds channel subscribers and AdSense-for-video.
6. **Job posts.** $99 single, $349 for five, plus retained search.
7. **Donations.** PayPal, Stripe, Buy Me a Coffee and Ko-fi links, plus Alipay, WeChat and bank pledges.
8. **Exit:** a domain and site sale inquiry through web.works/contact, shown on every page.

**Bold path:** launch Chinese (`/zh/`) and French (`/fr/`) versions of the six pillar guides within 60 days, and sign two anchor partners — a Casablanca law firm and a Shenzhen forwarder — on exclusive lead deals before chasing AdSense scale. **Safe path:** English plus tools only, AdSense-first, and add leads once traffic passes 20K sessions a month.
