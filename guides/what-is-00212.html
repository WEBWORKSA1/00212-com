00212 & +212: what it means and how to spot scam calls

What 00212 means

+212 is Morocco's country calling code, assigned by the International Telecommunication Union (ITU). The “+” stands for the international access prefix of the country you are calling from. In mainland China, Europe, India and most of Africa that prefix is 00. So dialling 00 212 from Beijing, Paris or Casablanca's neighbours connects you to Morocco. From the USA or Canada the prefix is 011, so it becomes 011 212.

00212 and Chinese number culture

In Chinese number symbolism, 2 (二, èr) is linked to pairs and harmony (好事成双, “good things come in pairs”), and 0 (零) to wholeness and a fresh start. When telephone numbers are read aloud, 1 is usually pronounced yāo (幺), so 00212 is spoken líng líng èr yāo èr. Unlike 8 (prosperity) or 520 (“I love you”), though, 00212 has no established traditional meaning. Its real importance for Chinese people is practical. It is the dialling route to a country Chinese citizens can visit without a visa, where Chinese companies are investing billions and which buys around $10B a year of Chinese goods. The same five characters also show up as an unrelated Hong Kong stock code, which has no connection to this site.

The +212 missed-call scam (wangiri)

Wangiri (Japanese for “one ring and cut”) is a fraud in which automated systems ring thousands of phones once from a foreign number. Curious recipients call back and are routed to premium-rate or international lines that bill by the minute. Many consumer-protection alerts name +212 among the codes used. Often the scammers are not in Morocco at all: the numbers can be spoofed or leased.

Key point: the country code is not the problem. The pattern is. An unexpected one-ring call, urgent requests for money, links, verification codes or “job offers” that ask you to pay are the red flags, whatever the number.

7 rules to stay safe

  1. Never call back an unknown international missed call.
  2. Never share one-time codes (OTP / 验证码), even with “your bank”.
  3. Don't click links in unexpected SMS, WhatsApp or WeChat messages.
  4. Verify callers through official numbers you look up yourself.
  5. Ask your operator to bar premium-rate or international calls if you never need them.
  6. Block and report the number in your phone app. In mainland China you can report to 12321 (spam) and 96110 (anti-fraud).
  7. Warn older relatives. They are targeted most often.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is 00212 the same as +212?

Yes. “+” stands for your country's international access prefix. In China and most of Europe that prefix is 00, so +212 = 00 212.

What is 00212 in China?

Dialled from mainland China, 00 212 connects to Morocco. The digits have no special traditional meaning in Chinese numerology; their significance is the telephone route to Morocco.

What should I do after a +212 missed call?

Don't call back. Block the number, and report it in your phone app or with our report form.
