Morocco visa-free travel guide for Chinese citizens

By the 00212 Editorial Desk · Updated 2026-09-28 · 8 min read

Entry rules (入境)

Morocco has let holders of ordinary PRC passports enter without a visa since June 2016, for tourist and short business stays (typically up to 90 days). Carry a passport valid for at least 6 months, a return ticket and your hotel bookings. Always recheck the latest rules with your airline or the Moroccan embassy before you fly.

The policy had a large effect. Chinese arrivals rose from about 10,000 a year before the waiver to roughly 141,000 in 2019, and to about 200,000 in 2025. Morocco is targeting 500,000 or more.

Flights (航班)

Direct services have linked Beijing and Casablanca, and new Shanghai routes have been reported. One-stop options via Doha, Dubai, Istanbul, Paris or Madrid are common and often cheaper.

Money (货币与支付)

  • Currency: Moroccan dirham (MAD). Exchange at banks or official bureaus and keep the receipts.
  • Cards work in hotels, malls and chain restaurants. Use cash in souks, taxis and small cafés.
  • Alipay and WeChat Pay acceptance is growing in tourist businesses, but don't rely on it.

Connectivity (电话与网络)

Buy a SIM at the airport (Maroc Telecom, Orange or inwi) with your passport, or install an eSIM before you leave. Google Maps and WhatsApp work normally in Morocco. Save your contacts' numbers in +212 format.

Etiquette & safety (礼仪与安全)

  • Dress modestly in medinas and at religious sites. Most mosques are closed to non-Muslims; Hassan II Mosque in Casablanca offers guided tours.
  • Ask before photographing people.
  • Bargaining is expected in souks. Start at about half the first price and stay friendly.
  • Use licensed guides and official taxis. Politely decline “friendly” strangers who offer directions.
  • Emergency numbers: police 19, ambulance/fire 15. Keep the Chinese Embassy in Rabat's details at hand.
