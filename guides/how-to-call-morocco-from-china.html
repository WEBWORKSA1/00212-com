How to call Morocco from China — and back

By the 00212 Editorial Desk · Updated 2026-09-28 · 4 min read

China → Morocco in 3 steps

  1. Dial 00, China's international access prefix. On a mobile you can type + instead.
  2. Dial 212, Morocco's country code.
  3. Dial the Moroccan number without its leading 0: 9 digits starting with 6 or 7 (mobile) or 5 (landline).

Example: the Moroccan mobile 06 12 34 56 78 becomes 00 212 6 12 34 56 78.

Morocco → China

Dial 00 86, then a mobile number (11 digits starting with 1) or the city code without its 0 plus the landline number. Examples: Beijing 00 86 10 XXXX XXXX, Shanghai 00 86 21 XXXX XXXX.

Time difference

China (UTC+8) is usually 7 hours ahead of Morocco (GMT+1). During Ramadan, when Morocco moves to GMT+0, the gap is 8 hours. The overlap window is short. 09:00–11:00 in Morocco equals 16:00–18:00 in Beijing, which suits business calls. See live clocks →

Cheapest ways to connect

  • Internet calls: WeChat works in both countries. Many Moroccans prefer WhatsApp, which needs a VPN in mainland China.
  • International call packs from Chinese carriers if you call regularly.
  • Local SIM or eSIM in Morocco (Maroc Telecom, Orange, inwi) for local calls and data.
  • VoIP numbers for businesses that need a Moroccan presence.
