Classic route

7-day first-timer itinerary

  1. Day 1 — Casablanca: arrive, Hassan II Mosque, Corniche.
  2. Day 2 — Rabat → Chefchaouen: capital highlights, drive to the blue town.
  3. Day 3 — Fès: guided medina walk, Chouara tannery.
  4. Day 4 — Middle Atlas → Merzouga: Ifrane cedar forests, sunset camel ride.
  5. Day 5 — Todra Gorge → Dades Valley: kasbahs and canyons.
  6. Day 6 — Aït Ben Haddou → Marrakech: UNESCO ksar, Tizi n'Tichka pass.
  7. Day 7 — Marrakech: Majorelle Garden, souks, Jemaa el-Fna night market.

Practical essentials

TopicWhat to know
EntryVisa-free for ordinary PRC passports for short stays; passport valid for 6+ months recommended.
MoneyMoroccan dirham (MAD). Cards in hotels and malls; cash in medinas and taxis. Convert CNY⇄MAD.
Phone & dataBuy a local SIM (Maroc Telecom, Orange, inwi) at the airport with your passport, or an eSIM before departure.
LanguageArabic and Amazigh are official; French is widely used; English is common in tourism.
TimeUsually 7 hours behind Beijing (GMT+1, GMT+0 during Ramadan). Live clocks.
EtiquetteDress modestly in medinas and religious sites; ask before photographing people; bargaining is normal in souks.
SafetyGenerally safe for tourists; watch for fake guides and pickpockets in crowded areas. Emergency: police 19, ambulance/fire 15.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do Chinese citizens need a visa for Morocco?

Holders of ordinary Chinese (PRC) passports have been able to enter Morocco without a visa since 2016 for short stays (typically up to 90 days). Rules can change — confirm with the Moroccan embassy or your airline before travel.

Is there a direct flight from China to Morocco?

Direct services between Beijing and Casablanca have operated, and new routes to Shanghai have been reported. Many travellers also connect via Doha, Dubai, Istanbul or Paris.

Can I use Alipay or WeChat Pay in Morocco?

Acceptance is growing in tourist businesses but is not universal. Carry a Visa/Mastercard/UnionPay card and some Moroccan dirhams (MAD) in cash.

What is the best time to visit Morocco?

Spring (March–May) and autumn (September–November) are the most comfortable. Summer is very hot inland; winter nights in the desert and mountains are cold.
